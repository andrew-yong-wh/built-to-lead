Architecture of the Program

01.

Practice > Theory Each week opens with a real problem from their own work. The framework is the tool that solves it.

02.

The Spacing Effect Learning distributed across seven weeks is retained far better than content massed into two days.

03.

40% Application Ratio The strongest single predictor of behaviour change. More doing, less listening.

04.

The Weekly Question Anonymous, commentary-driven email problem solving between sessions.

05.

Cohort of 12 Every manager speaks, brings own situations, and gets 2 hours of 1:1 time.

06.

Manager Integration Their leader is involved at the start, at Week 7, and at 90 days.
