“Ready is a result, not a requirement.”

A 3-minute self-check across the five things a manager is actually built from  ·  by Andrew WH

Most people are handed a team and told to lead — with no system underneath them. So they run on instinct and effort. It works, until the day it doesn't.

This diagnostic doesn't grade you. It shows you where you stand right now across the five capabilities every effective manager is built from — and, more usefully, where your biggest lever is. Readiness isn't something you wait to feel. It's something you build, deliberately, in a specific order.
