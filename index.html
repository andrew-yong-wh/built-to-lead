Three Pillars of a Manager

Functional

Takes the lead on their responsibilities. Accountable, and delegates with confidence.

Operational

Runs the operation with a steady rhythm. Priorities clear, commitments tracked, problems dealt with early.

Effective

Delivers the results the business is counting on, through the team, consistently.

What we contract on

We measure behaviour through their plans. Behaviour is what a manager does differently. It is baselined before the program, observed at 90 days, and verified by the person they report to.

Leadership reaches business performance through the decisions a manager makes and the way they operate with their team.

Why the design works

✦

Practice before theory. Each week opens with a real problem from their own work. The framework is the tool that solves it.

✦

The spacing effect. Learning distributed across seven weeks is retained far better than content massed into two days.

✦

High Application Ratio. More than 40% of hours are spent applying, not listening—the strongest predictor of behaviour change.

✦

The Weekly Question. Managers answer one leadership question by email between sessions. Responses return anonymously with commentary.

✦

Cohort of 12. Every manager speaks, brings their own situations, and receives two hours of one-to-one time.

✦

Manager Involvement. Their own manager is involved at the start, at Week 7, and at 90 days.
